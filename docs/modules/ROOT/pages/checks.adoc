= Checks
:description: Every check findable runs, by id.

Every finding has a check id, a severity, the pages it was seen on and a fix.
`ignore:` in the config skips a check by id prefix.

[cols="1,3"]
|===
| Id prefix | What it looks at

| `meta.title.*`, `meta.description.*` | Missing, too long or duplicated across pages.
| `meta.h1.*`, `meta.lang.missing` | One visible `<h1>`; a `lang` on `<html>`.
| `meta.canonical.*` | Missing, relative, or on another host; on a live crawl, a canonical other than the exact URL the page answers 200 at, trailing slash included (`meta.canonical.redirect`).
| `meta.title.brand` | With `brand:` or `business.name`, titles that do not name the brand: a warning on locale home pages, information elsewhere.
| `index.noindex`, `index.noindex.listed` | A page that asks not to be indexed but is not listed in `noindex:`; a noindex page in the sitemap.
| `links.broken`, `links.orphan`, `links.redirect` | Internal links to pages that do not exist; pages nothing links to; on a live crawl, links that reach their page only through a redirect.
| `img.alt.missing`, `social.og.missing` | Images without `alt`; no share card.
| `content.thin` | Fewer words than `min_words`.
| `content.query`, `content.query.page` | Each of `queries:`: its words missing from the page's title or h1 (warning) or description (information); no page at its path.
| `sitemap.*` | Missing or invalid (`.xml.gz` is read too); URLs on another host, unknown, not canonical; on a live crawl, URLs that answer an error, redirect or are not HTML (`sitemap.url.status`); indexable pages left out; on a live crawl, URLs that answer only after a redirect, such as one that adds a trailing slash (`sitemap.url.redirect`).
| `redirect.temporary` | Live crawl only: sitemap URLs that reach their page through a 302, 303 or 307.
| `crawl.*` | Live crawl only: pages robots.txt keeps the `findable` token out of (`crawl.skipped`), and a crawl cut short by `max_pages` (`crawl.truncated`).
| `robots.*` | Missing, blocking everything, or not naming the sitemap.
| `hreflang.*` | With two or more locales: missing, invalid, not reciprocal, no self link, no `x-default`; on a live crawl, alternates that redirect (`hreflang.redirect`).
| `schema.invalid.json`, `schema.expected` | JSON-LD that does not parse; types the config requires on a path.
| `schema.organization.fields`, `schema.organization.sameas`, `schema.localbusiness.fields` | Name, url, logo, and `sameAs` profiles; address and telephone.
| `schema.website.name` | With a brand set: a locale home page without a WebSite block named for it, which Google reads for the site name in results.
| `schema.jobposting.*` | Required and recommended fields, a `validThrough` already past (informational: it is one way Google accepts to close a job), JobPosting on a listing page.
| `schema.breadcrumb.*` | Items and positions.
| `local.*` | No Organization or LocalBusiness anywhere; a business name or phone in the JSON-LD that differs from `business:`.
| `ai.crawler.*` | robots.txt against `ai_crawlers:` for each kind of AI crawler.
| `ai.llms.*` | llms.txt missing, malformed, or linking to pages that do not exist.
|===

== AI crawlers

[cols="1,3"]
|===
| Kind | User agents

| `search` | OAI-SearchBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot
| `user` | ChatGPT-User, Claude-User, Perplexity-User
| `training` | GPTBot, ClaudeBot, CCBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, Meta-ExternalAgent, Bytespider
|===

robots.txt is read as RFC 9309 says: a group naming the agent replaces the `*`
group, and the longest matching rule wins.
