# Content plan

## 1. Who searches, for what

Ask the owner for the audiences and what each one is trying to do. For each audience list the questions they type, in their words and their languages. Sources, best first:

1. Search Console queries the site already shows for (the `measure` skill).
2. The questions sales and support answer every week.
3. Google's "People also ask" and autocomplete, and the questions people ask AI assistants.
4. Competitors' pages that rank.

Do not invent search volumes. Without data, rank by how close a query is to a sale.

## 2. One page per intent

Group the questions by intent and give each group one page:

| Intent | Page |
|---|---|
| "who does X in Y" | a service or location page |
| "how much / how long / what do I need" | a guide with the answer first |
| "X vs Y" | a comparison with a table |
| a specific job, product, event | its own page with its structured data |

Two pages for one intent compete with each other: merge them, redirect the weaker one.

## 3. Clusters and links

A hub page per topic links to every page in the cluster, and each links back and to its neighbours. findable's `links.orphan` lists pages nothing links to.

## 4. Order

Write first what is closest to revenue and where the site has the best chance: service and location pages, then the guides that support them, then news. Refresh pages that lost clicks before writing new ones.

## 5. Each page

The answer first, then the detail. Facts with sources. Who wrote it. A clear next step (the `cro` skill). A title and description that say what the page offers (findable checks them). No AI-generated filler: Google's scaled content abuse policy targets pages made to rank rather than to help.

Hand the plan to the owner as a table: page, audience, question, intent, priority, status.
