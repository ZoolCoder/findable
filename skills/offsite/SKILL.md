# Off-site reach

Search engines and AI answers trust what other sites say about a business more than what it says about itself. The work is to deserve mentions and then ask for them.

## Assets worth linking to

Pick one or two the business can make better than anyone:

- data it alone has (salary ranges by sector, placement numbers, survey results), published yearly;
- a guide that answers a hard question fully (visa steps, licensing, costs);
- a free tool or calculator;
- real stories with names and photos, with consent.

## Where mentions come from

| Source | How |
|---|---|
| Partners, clients, suppliers | a case study or "our partners" page on their site; ask |
| Industry associations, chambers of commerce | membership listings |
| Local and trade press | send the data asset or a story with a hook; answer journalist requests |
| Universities, training centres | careers and placement resources |
| Directories | the few that matter in the country and industry, same NAP everywhere |
| Social profiles | official LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube; link them from Organization `sameAs` |
| Communities | answer questions where the audience asks them; disclose who you are |

Track what was sent, to whom, and the outcome in a sheet. Follow up once.

## Never

- Buy links, sell links, or trade links in bulk. Paid placements carry `rel="sponsored"`.
- Use private blog networks, link farms, automated comment or forum links, or expired-domain redirects.
- Post as a fake customer or hide that a post comes from the business.
- Send outreach email on the owner's behalf without their yes to each message.
